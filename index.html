Published Research Paper (Collaborative Work) Armenian Scientific Contribution

Investigating Ground State Properties of Lithium Hydride (LiH) Using Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE)

Abstract Summary: Simulating molecules at the quantum level is vital for materials science, energy research, and drug development. Classical computers struggle with these simulations because quantum states grow exponentially. Traditional approximations lose accuracy for complex systems with strong particle interactions.

To overcome this, our collaborative research investigates the ground state properties of Lithium Hydride (LiH) using the Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE). VQE creates a hybrid feedback loop between a quantum computer and a classical optimizer: the quantum hardware acts as a physical model generating trial energy states, while the classical computer analyzes the energy score and tunes parameters to find the absolute minimum energy level.

With only four electrons, LiH serves as an ideal, crucial molecule for testing early-stage quantum hardware, bridging light hydrogen and heavier lithium properties to pave the way for complex molecular simulations in clean energy and medicine.
